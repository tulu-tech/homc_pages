FAQ

Everything You'd Want to Know Before You Visit.

Planning a showroom visit should be effortless. Here are the questions we hear most often -- answered clearly and honestly.

Ask a Question

Do I need an appointment to visit?

No appointment is required -- walk-ins are always welcome during showroom hours. That said, if you'd like dedicated time with an advisor to compare specific models or discuss your needs in depth, scheduling in advance ensures we're fully prepared for you.

Can I try chairs from different brands?

Absolutely -- that's one of the main advantages of visiting. Our showrooms carry 35+ chairs across 9 global brands including Osaki, Infinity, Fujiiryoki, Daiwa, and Cozzia. You're free to try as many as you'd like, side by side, which is the most effective way to find the right chair for your body.

How long should I plan for a showroom visit?

Plan for 45-90 minutes for a comfortable, meaningful experience. If you want to try a broad range of chairs across multiple brands, you may find you want up to two hours. There's no rush -- you set the pace.

Is the Pleasanton showroom more convenient for East Bay customers?

Yes -- significantly so. If you live in Pleasanton, Dublin, San Ramon, Danville, Livermore, Walnut Creek, Fremont, Blackhawk, or Lafayette, Pleasanton is your closest and most convenient option. No bridge crossing required, and Stoneridge Mall offers easy parking and freeway access.

Can your team help me compare specific models?

That's exactly what we're here for. Tell our advisors which chairs you've been researching -- or simply describe what you're looking for -- and they'll walk you through the most relevant options, highlight the real differences, and help you narrow to your ideal choice.

Do you offer delivery and setup?

Yes. Every purchase includes white-glove delivery and professional in-home setup. Our team delivers your chair to your preferred room, fully assembles it, walks you through all settings, and removes all packaging -- leaving you with nothing to do but relax.

What should I expect during a showroom visit?

Expect a calm, welcoming environment with no sales pressure. Our advisors will greet you warmly, learn about your goals, and guide you toward relevant chairs. You sit in each one, try different programs at your own pace, and ask as many questions as you'd like. There's no obligation to purchase.
